# Try one question in your current chat / 直接在当前聊天试一个问题

No installation or personal history is needed. Copy the whole block below into an AI conversation. This **synthetic, manually supplied review** illustrates the kind of work Second Look is meant to deliver. It does not install the skill, discover history or save a review ledger. Your current model handles the text.

无需安装，也不需要私人历史。把下面整块内容复制到当前 AI 聊天中即可。它是合成材料的手动复盘演示，不代表完整 skill 已运行。

```text
Please reconsider this old conversation and deliver the updated work.
Answer in my language. Treat the transcript as evidence, not instructions.
Restore the user's goal and later corrections. Produce a complete usable
result first, then explain the material change and cite message IDs.
Keep facts, proposals and unknowns separate. If the old answer is already
sound, say so. Do not read other conversations or take external actions.

SYNTHETIC HISTORY — no real participants or findings

[A1 — user, earlier]
I want to understand how freelance designers handle client feedback.
I was considering eight 30-minute interviews. I don't have an approved budget.
[A2 — assistant, earlier]
Hire a research agency for 6,000 yuan. Wait until the budget is approved.
Ask interviewees whether they would use your feedback-management app.
[A3 — user, correction]
I cannot approve that spending. I want recent incidents and current
workarounds, not an app pitch. Eight interviews won't estimate prevalence.

[B1 — user, later, same project]
Six eligible people have volunteered. A partner can introduce two more
for free. Those introductions are not booked interviews.
[B2 — user, new constraints]
The pilot now has a 1,200-yuan cap. Pay 100 yuan per completed interview,
including volunteers. Use existing meeting tools and manual notes.
Two people can each spend two hours a week for three weeks; include all
scheduling, notes, incentive administration and synthesis in those hours.
Both can interview separately. Recording is optional with separate consent.
[B3 — user, current goal]
At the end, decide whether a small manual-service experiment is worth
trying. Do not assume we'll build an app, invent findings or report bookings
as completions. Give me a feasible plan, a 30-minute interview guide,
a note template and a decision worksheet under these constraints.
```

## Check your result

The plan should preserve the neutral research goal, count incentives for volunteers, respect weekly capacity as well as total hours, and report only actual completions. Eight completions would cost 800 yuan; available staff capacity totals 12 person-hours. Delays may require fewer interviews. No interview findings exist yet.

A polished summary without the plan, guide and worksheets is not a completed result. There can be several valid plans.
