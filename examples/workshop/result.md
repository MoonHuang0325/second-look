# A ready-to-use 45-minute workshop

**Supported improvement:** preserve the 15-minute pair exercise and 10-minute Q&A while giving every participant a source-checking task.

| Time | Activity | Instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 | Introduction | Explain the goal: check a consequential AI claim against its source. |
| 3–10 | Demonstration | Show an answer, a claim, and its primary source. Separate source support from inference. |
| 10–25 | Pair exercise | Form six pairs. Give each pair two claims with sources; each participant checks one, then they compare evidence. |
| 25–35 | Q&A | Discuss inaccessible sources, outdated evidence, and conclusions that exceed the evidence. |
| 35–45 | Debrief | Three pairs share for two minutes each; use four minutes to consolidate a repeatable checking method. No homework. |
