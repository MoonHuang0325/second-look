# Draft a shareable result only when requested

Help the user tell the story of a result they choose. Return a draft for their review, never a post or a message sent to another person.

Ask what may be public only when that cannot be inferred from the user's current instructions. Default to a generalized description of the problem and a compact before/after result. Omit personal names, account/thread links, local paths, client details, sensitive numbers, transcript quotations and attachments unless specifically approved for that audience. Recheck the proposed draft against those categories; paraphrasing alone is not anonymization.

Label synthetic, reconstructed, user-reported and directly verified material accurately. Never invent adoption, saved time, ROI, testimonials or model-superiority claims. Mention limits that change how a reader would interpret the result. A sample layout:

- The question that had stalled.
- What new evidence or correction justified a second look.
- The usable result and how it was checked.
- What remains uncertain.

Include the project's name or link only if the user wants it in the draft. Do not insert promotional footers in ordinary review results. A share draft is not permission to persist public copies of the user's private material.
